Battery Pack Accessories RFQ Guide for Europe

A specification-first checklist for buyers consolidating cylindrical-cell holders, conductive connections, insulation materials and flexible wire into one qualified sourcing request.

01

Why this page exists

02

Consolidated sourcing scope

Four accessory groups for one pack BOM.

PACK STRUCTURECylindrical cell holders

Listings reference 18650, 21700, 26650, 32650 and 33140 formats. Confirm exact cavity size, pitch, resin, layout and tolerance.

 CONDUCTIVE CONNECTIONNickel strip and custom connections

Confirm pure nickel versus plated material, composition evidence, thickness, width, pitch, burr control and welding process.

 INSULATIONFish paper, heat shrink and tapes

Confirm substrate, thickness, adhesive system, temperature or voltage requirement, die-cut drawing and applicable report scope.

 PACK WIRINGFlexible silicone wire

Confirm conductor material, gauge, strand construction, jacket rating, color, cut length and required electrical documentation.
03

Copy into your RFQ

Minimum fields for a comparable offer.

  • Cell format, cell drawing and pack S/P layout
  • Part drawing, revision and acceptable tolerance
  • Exact material grade and proof required
  • Electrical current or insulation design requirement
  • Welding, bonding or assembly process
  • Prototype/sample quantity and approval test
  • Trial order and forecast annual volume
  • Packaging, labeling and traceability
  • Destination country and Incoterm request
  • Document list with product and batch scope

Do not treat a management-system certificate as a blanket product certificate. Verify holder, report number, validity, product model and test scope for any CE, RoHS, REACH or other document requested.

04

European buyer checks

Approval should connect specification, sample and batch.

  1. Freeze the drawing.Record revision, material, dimensional tolerances and mating cell information.
  2. Approve a representative sample.Test fit, weldability, conductivity or insulation performance under the buyer's validated process.
  3. Define incoming inspection.Agree sampling, measurement method, material evidence, lot traceability and packaging acceptance.
  4. Reconfirm delivery evidence.MOQ, sample terms, production lead time, logistics and document scope remain subject to the current quotation.

FAQ

Questions procurement teams ask first.

What should be included in the RFQ?+

Cell and pack data, drawings, exact material and performance requirements, sample and annual quantities, destination, packaging, traceability and the required document list.

Can NanXing quote multiple accessory categories?+

NanXing publishes sourcing paths for holders, nickel and copper connections, heat shrink, fish paper, tapes, fiberglass sheet and silicone wire. Quote and approve each part against its own specification.

Are commercial terms fixed here?+

No. Confirm price, MOQ, samples, lead time, capacity allocation and document coverage on the current Alibaba.com listing or directly with Alma Han.
