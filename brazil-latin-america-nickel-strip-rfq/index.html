Battery Nickel Strip RFQ and Material Verification Guide

A specification-first checklist for buyers comparing nickel strip and custom battery connection quotations for cylindrical-cell packs.

01

Buyer intent

Compare the material and drawing, not only the price per roll.

03

Copy into the RFQ

Ten fields that make quotations comparable.

  • Required material: pure nickel, plated material or other
  • Material grade and evidence required
  • Thickness, width and length or roll format
  • Cell type, pack S/P layout and center distance
  • Part drawing, revision and dimensional tolerance
  • Spot-welding equipment and validated process window
  • Prototype quantity and approval tests
  • Trial order and forecast volume
  • Packaging, labeling and batch traceability
  • Destination, requested Incoterm and delivery window

Do not infer composition from surface color or a generic product name. Tie material evidence and inspection records to the exact quoted item and production lot.

04

Material proof ladder

Use evidence tied to the exact item and lot.

  1. Written designation.Record the requested material construction, part number and drawing revision.
  2. Lot-tied record.Request supplier or composition records that identify the quoted item and production lot.
  3. Representative approval.Approve a coupon or sample using the buyer's validated welding and test process.
  4. Production control.Define incoming inspection, traceability and change-notification requirements.

Informal magnet, salt-water or household chemical checks are not adequate purchase-release evidence. Material identity and pack suitability should be validated through documented, qualified methods.

05

Sample approval

Connect incoming evidence to pack-level performance.

  1. Freeze the specification.Record the drawing revision, material, dimensions, edge requirements and packaging format.
  2. Test representative samples.Use qualified personnel and the buyer's validated welding, resistance, pull and temperature-rise methods as applicable.
  3. Define incoming inspection.Agree measurement method, material records, sampling plan, lot traceability and rejection handling.
  4. Reconfirm commercial fields.Verify current MOQ, sample terms, price, lead time, logistics and document scope before placing an order.
06

FAQ / Perguntas frequentes

Questions buyers should settle before sampling.

Qual a diferença entre níquel puro e aço niquelado?+

They are different material constructions. Specify and verify the exact material required by the pack design; appearance or a generic listing name is not sufficient purchase-release evidence.

Como comprovar o material da fita de níquel?+

Request composition evidence tied to the quoted item and production lot, validate a representative sample with the buyer's approved welding and test process, and define incoming inspection.

MOQ, prazo e preço estão fixos?+

No. Confirm current MOQ, sample terms, lead time, price and document coverage on the live NanXing Alibaba.com listing or directly with Alma Han.
