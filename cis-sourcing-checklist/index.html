A useful battery-accessory inquiry does more than name a cell size. It connects pack geometry, current path, insulation requirements, production quantity and destination to a traceable product listing.

01 / RFQ inputs

Define the pack before selecting the accessory.

01Cell format and layout

State the exact cylindrical-cell format, series/parallel layout and pack drawing. Do not rely only on a generic label such as “18650 holder.”

02Material and dimensions

Specify the required material, thickness, width, pitch, hole pattern, tolerance and surface condition. Mark every unknown as “supplier to confirm.”

03Electrical and insulation need

Provide the intended current path, joining process and insulation location. Qualified engineers should confirm suitability for the actual pack.

04Quantity and destination

Separate sample quantity from expected production volume and name the final destination, consignee and requested delivery timing.

03 / Russia & CIS compliance

Screen the transaction—not just the product name.

Battery holders, metal strips, insulation and wire cannot be classified for export control from a category name alone. Before accepting an order, identify the exact item, technical parameters, material origin, buyer, beneficial owner, consignee, freight route, end user and end use.

  1. Classify the item.Check the 2026 Chinese dual-use catalogue and applicable customs declaration requirements against the exact specification.
  2. Screen every party.Review buyer, consignee, beneficial owner, bank, freight forwarder and final user against applicable restricted-party lists.
  3. Document end use.Obtain a credible civilian application statement. Escalate military, aerospace, drone, surveillance or unclear end-use signals for specialist review.
  4. Check origin-based rules.If the product, equipment, software or payment chain includes controlled US-, EU- or other-origin content, assess the rules of those jurisdictions.
  5. Keep an audit trail.Retain classification, screening results, end-use documents, invoice, packing list, payment and shipping evidence.

04 / FAQ

Questions to close before quotation.

What information should a battery-pack RFQ include?+

State the cell format, pack layout, drawing, material, dimensions and tolerances, target quantity, destination and required supporting documents.

Are all battery pack accessories export-controlled?+

No general conclusion should be made from the product category alone. Classification depends on the exact item, technical parameters, origin, destination, parties, end user and end use.
