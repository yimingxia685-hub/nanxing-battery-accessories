02 / RFQ fields

Give the supplier an approval target.

01Cell drawing

Send the manufacturer drawing or measured diameter, length, sleeve thickness and terminal details. Name the chemistry and final application separately.

02Pack layout

State series/parallel arrangement, cell count, holder orientation, pitch, spacing, cooling path and enclosure clearance.

03Material requirement

Specify the approved polymer or performance requirement, operating temperature, flammability requirement and any evidence your project requires.

04Dimensions and tolerance

Attach a controlled drawing with cavity diameter, pitch, overall size, locking features, flatness and critical tolerances.

05Sample approval

Define sample quantity, mating parts, fit check, assembly method and acceptance record before volume production.

06Commercial details

Separate prototype and production quantities. State destination, target date, packaging limits and required shipping documents.

03 / Incoming inspection

Inspect the features that control assembly.

  1. Identity and revision.Match the part number, drawing revision, color and lot information to the approved order.
  2. Critical dimensions.Measure cavity size, center pitch, overall geometry and locking features using the agreed sampling plan.
  3. Fit and handling.Check insertion force, retention, orientation, sharp edges, flash, warpage and damage using approved cells or gauges.
  4. Pack validation.Validate vibration, thermal behavior, insulation and assembly performance in the actual battery-pack design.

Battery-pack design and assembly can involve high current, stored energy, heat and fire risk. Use qualified engineering personnel and validate the complete pack for its intended application.

04 / FAQ

Questions to close before purchase.

What should a cylindrical battery holder RFQ include?+

State the exact cell drawing, measured diameter, pack layout, holder quantity, material requirement, spacing, tolerance, operating environment, inspection method, order quantity and destination.

Can the cell format name alone confirm fit?+

No. Confirm the actual cell drawing, sleeve and terminal details, cavity dimensions, pitch, orientation and assembly clearance with an approved sample.
