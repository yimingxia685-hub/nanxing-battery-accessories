02 / RFQ fields

Send enough detail for a traceable quotation.

01Cell and pack geometry

State the cell format, exact diameter, series/parallel arrangement, cell pitch and approved drawing revision.

02Material identity

Specify pure nickel, nickel-plated steel or another approved material. Add grade, composition evidence and coating requirements where applicable.

03Dimensions and tolerance

Define thickness, width, length, hole or slot geometry, burr direction, flatness and dimensional tolerances.

04Joining and application

Name the spot-, laser- or ultrasonic-welding process, current path and final application. Engineering approval remains the buyer's responsibility.

05Samples and inspection

Separate sample quantity from production volume. State incoming-inspection, peel-test, resistance or composition-report needs.

06Delivery and documents

Provide destination, requested Incoterm, packaging limits, target date and required invoice, packing and material documents.

03 / Buyer decision

Do not select strip thickness from a universal amp chart.

Pack current, conductor geometry, temperature rise, welding quality, cell spacing, duty cycle, cooling and enclosure conditions interact. Use design calculations and validation tests for the actual pack. A sample that welds cleanly is not, by itself, proof of current-carrying suitability.

  1. Approve the drawing.Match every dimension and tolerance to the pack layout and holder pitch.
  2. Verify the material.Reconcile the quotation, label, batch document and independent test requirement before volume production.
  3. Validate the process.Set and document the welding window, joint inspection and electrical/thermal acceptance criteria.
  4. Control revisions.Use a part number and revision so samples and production lots remain traceable.

04 / Ukraine transaction checks

Add route and end-use screening to the RFQ.

For shipments involving Ukraine or neighboring transit routes, document the buyer, beneficial owner, consignee, freight route, final user, civilian end use and payment chain. Screen the exact parties and goods under the rules applicable to the transaction. Escalate military, drone, surveillance, aerospace or unclear end-use indicators for specialist review.

NanXing does not support false declarations, concealed end users or sanctions and export-control evasion. This page is general sourcing information, not legal advice.

05 / FAQ

Questions to close before ordering.

What should a nickel-strip RFQ include?+

Include the cell format, series and parallel layout, drawing, material grade, thickness, width, pitch, tolerance, welding process, quantity, destination and required batch documents.

How should pure nickel versus nickel-plated steel be confirmed?+

Do not rely on the title alone. Request the exact material designation, composition evidence, batch certificate or test report, and an approved sample that matches the purchase specification.
